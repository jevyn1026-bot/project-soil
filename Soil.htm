How You Can Help

Follow three simple steps to report issues and earn green incentives.

1

Snap & Pin

Take a clear photo of suspected soil pollution and select its exact location on our map.

2

Describe

Provide details about the type of pollutant (waste dumping, chemical spill, etc.) and submit.

3

Earn Rewards

Once city officials verify your report, you receive Eco-Points redeemable for local rewards!

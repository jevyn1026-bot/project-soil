About ProjectSUN

ProjectSUN (Soil Pollution Monitoring and Environmental Reporting System) is a web-based platform designed to help citizens report, monitor, and reduce soil pollution throughout Pasig City using technology, community participation, and environmental responsibility.

What is ProjectSUN?

ProjectSUN (Soil Pollution Monitoring and Environmental Reporting System) is a web-based environmental reporting platform that enables citizens to report, monitor, and help reduce soil pollution throughout Pasig City. The system combines GPS location tracking, interactive mapping, photo documentation, and real-time reporting to improve communication between the community and local government agencies. By providing accurate environmental data, ProjectSUN supports faster response to pollution incidents, promotes environmental awareness, and encourages public participation in protecting natural resources for present and future generations.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To empower communities by providing an accessible and reliable digital platform for reporting soil pollution, promoting environmental responsibility, and supporting government agencies in protecting public health and the environment.

Vision

To become a trusted environmental monitoring platform that contributes to a cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable Pasig City through innovation, transparency, and active community participation.

Core Features

ProjectSUN provides modern digital tools that enable citizens and local government agencies to monitor, report, and manage soil pollution incidents more efficiently.

GPS Location Tracking

Automatically records the exact location of every report to improve mapping accuracy and speed up environmental response.

Photo Evidence Upload

Users can attach photos to provide visual evidence that helps verify pollution incidents.

Interactive Pollution Map

Displays reported pollution locations across Pasig City, allowing users and administrators to identify affected areas.

Analytics Dashboard

Presents report statistics, pollution trends, and environmental data to support informed decision-making.

Report Status Monitoring

Tracks submitted reports from pending review to resolved, promoting transparency and accountability.

Government Administration

Provides authorized personnel with tools to review, manage, and update reports through a secure administration panel.

Pollution Classification Guide

Selecting the correct pollution category helps local government authorities understand environmental problems, prioritize response efforts, and maintain accurate environmental records. Before submitting a report, review the categories below and choose the one that best matches the pollution incident.

Illegal Waste Dumping

The unauthorized disposal of household, commercial, or industrial waste in vacant lots, roadsides, sidewalks, riversides, and other public areas.

Examples:
  • Household garbage
  • Mixed waste piles
  • Construction waste
  • Illegal dumping sites

Plastic Waste

Accumulation of plastic materials that remain in the environment for long periods and contribute to soil pollution.

Examples:
  • Plastic bottles
  • Plastic bags
  • Food wrappers
  • Packaging materials

Chemical Pollution

Soil contamination caused by hazardous chemicals released from homes, laboratories, businesses, or industrial facilities.

Examples:
  • Paint
  • Solvents
  • Pesticides
  • Industrial chemicals

Oil and Fuel Spill

Petroleum products leaking into the soil, causing environmental contamination and potential health hazards.

Examples:
  • Gasoline spills
  • Diesel leaks
  • Engine oil
  • Lubricants

Construction Debris

Improper disposal of construction materials after building, renovation, or demolition activities.

Examples:
  • Concrete
  • Bricks
  • Wood
  • Metal scraps

Agricultural Waste

Waste produced by farming activities that may negatively affect soil quality and nearby ecosystems.

Examples:
  • Fertilizers
  • Pesticides
  • Livestock waste
  • Agricultural chemicals

Electronic Waste (E-Waste)

Discarded electronic devices and accessories containing hazardous materials that require proper disposal.

Examples:
  • Computers
  • Mobile phones
  • Batteries
  • Circuit boards

Industrial Waste

Waste generated from factories and manufacturing facilities that may contaminate surrounding land and water.

Examples:
  • Industrial sludge
  • Metal residues
  • Ash
  • Factory by-products

Hazardous Waste

Dangerous materials that pose significant risks to human health and the environment if improperly handled.

Examples:
  • Medical waste
  • Toxic chemicals
  • Contaminated containers
  • Hazardous batteries

Other Environmental Concerns

Environmental issues that do not fit the listed categories but still require government investigation and appropriate action.

Examples:
  • Unknown pollutants
  • Suspicious contaminated soil
  • Mixed pollution sources
  • Other environmental hazards

How to Submit a Report

Submitting a soil pollution report through ProjectSUN is quick and simple. Follow these steps to help local authorities identify, verify, and respond to environmental concerns in Pasig City.

Step 1

Enable Your Location

Allow ProjectSUN to access your GPS location so the exact location of the pollution incident can be recorded accurately.

Step 2

Select a Pollution Type

Choose the pollution category that best matches the environmental issue you observed using the Pollution Classification Guide.

Step 3

Provide Report Details

Describe the pollution incident clearly, including any important information that may help authorities understand the situation.

Step 4

Upload a Photo

Attach a clear photo of the pollution incident to provide visual evidence and assist with report verification.

Step 5

5z

Submit the Report

Review the information you entered and submit your report. It will be sent to the ProjectSUN system for review by authorized administrators.

Step 6

Track Report Status

After submission, administrators will review your report. Its status will be updated as it progresses through the verification process.

Why Community Participation Matters

Protecting the environment is a shared responsibility. ProjectSUN encourages citizens to actively participate in identifying and reporting soil pollution incidents throughout Pasig City. Every report submitted contributes valuable information that helps local government agencies understand environmental conditions and respond more effectively.

Community participation improves the accuracy of environmental monitoring by allowing pollution incidents to be reported quickly and from different locations. Through active public involvement, ProjectSUN helps identify pollution hotspots, supports faster investigation, and promotes better environmental planning.

By working together, citizens and government agencies can help reduce soil pollution, protect natural resources, improve public health, and create a cleaner, safer, and more sustainable Pasig City for present and future generations.
